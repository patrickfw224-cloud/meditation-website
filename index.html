Method

How a sitting works.

Three small decisions, then nothing to do but sit.

  1. 01

    Choose a practice

    Pick the door that fits tonight. Unsure? Breath is always a good first step.

  2. 02

    Pick a time

    Evening and late-night slots, every day. Join online or come to the studio.

  3. 03

    Sit with a guide

    Live, spoken guidance in a small group. Cameras off if you like. Stay after for tea.

Questions

Before you arrive.

Do I need any experience?

No. Every sitting is guided from the first breath to the last. If you can listen, you can do this. Breath and Grounding are the gentlest places to begin.

What happens in an online session?

You receive a link by email an hour before. Join from anywhere quiet. Cameras and microphones can stay off; the guide speaks, you listen, and there is a short optional chat at the end.

What should I bring to the studio?

Nothing. We have cushions, chairs, blankets and tea. Wear whatever you can sit comfortably in for an hour. Arrive a few minutes early; the door closes at the start so nobody is disturbed.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, up to 12 hours before the sitting, from the booking page on the device you booked with, or by replying to your confirmation email. Later than that, the sitting is used.

Is there a mostly silent option?

Yes. The 10pm Grounding sitting is almost entirely silent, with a few words at the start and a bell at the end. Many people use it as the last thing they do before sleep.
